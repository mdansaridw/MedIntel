Problem Statement & Impact (20%)

Healthcare is Data-Rich but Insight-Blind

01.

Physician Burnout

Doctors spend 2 hours typing EHR notes for every 1 hour with patients. Unstructured notes trap vital clinical facts in dead text silos.

02.

The "SALAD" Hazard

Sound-Alike Look-Alike Drug confusions (e.g., Celebrex vs Celexa) cause 1.5M+ preventable medication injuries and fatal mix-ups annually.

03.

Supply Blindspot

Doctors prescribe treatments without knowing pharmacy stock levels, causing patient delays, emergency rationing, and inventory chaos.

Technical Complexity: Security Architecture (25%)

Zero-Trust Security & Two-Vault HIPAA De-identification

Vault 1: PII Tokenizer

Encrypted Relational Storage

  • • Encrypted at rest via AES-256-GCM using isolated keys.
  • • Holds: Real Name, SSN, Phone, Address, Date of Birth.
  • • Strips all 18 HIPAA Safe Harbor direct identifiers.
  • • Emits a one-way anonymous Token UUID (e.g. urn:uuid:7f3b...).
  • • Re-identified in memory ONLY for authorized attending clinicians.
Vault 2: De-identified PHI Graph

Neo4j Graph & ABAC

  • • Zero Personal Identifiers: Graph holds only anonymous tokens and clinical paths.
  • • Leak-Proof: Stealing the graph reveals NO identifiable patient names.
  • • Attribute-Based Access Control (ABAC): Dynamic policy evaluation (Doctor Dept vs Patient Care Team).
  • • Emergency Break-Glass: Instant override for ER trauma with tamper-evident audit logging.
Technical Complexity: Graph Intelligence (25%)

Knowledge Graph & Treatment Intelligence

Multi-Hop Reasoning

Why Neo4j Beats Vector DBs

Pure Vector DBs are black boxes that hallucinate dosages. Neo4j executes deterministic multi-hop traversals: Patient → Condition → Contraindicated Drug → Stocked Lot.

Biomarker Deltas (Δ)

Mathematical Treatment Efficacy

Mines longitudinal observations (HbA1c, Blood Pressure) before and after medication. Computes actual delta scores to prove which drug resolved the condition.

Hybrid Cohorts

Graph + Vector Patient Similarity

Combines demographic feature vectors (age, gender, risk) with graph topological overlap to discover twin patient cohorts and treatment success trajectories.

Safety Innovation (25%)

Proactive Safeguards: SALAD Errors & Supply Chain

Medication Safety Engine

SALAD Drug Error Prevention

  • • ISMP Sound-Alike Look-Alike Drug database integration.
  • • Double Metaphone Algorithm: Converts spoken names into phonetic keys to catch auditory prescribing confusion.
  • • Normalized Levenshtein Distance: Scans visual orthographic similarity against hospital formulary.
  • • Real-Time Alert: Warns when Celebrex (NSAID) might be confused with Celexa (Antidepressant) during verbal order.
Hospital Pharmacy Graph

Live Pharmacy Stock Verification

  • • Dynamic Supply Nodes: Links (:Medication) directly to (:PharmacyInventory).
  • • Point-of-Care Check: Validates inventory at the exact second of prescription.
  • • Shortage Alternatives: If stock is below threshold, suggests in-stock therapeutic equivalents.
  • • Instant Batch Recall: Traverses graph to identify all patients who took a tainted lot in a single query.
